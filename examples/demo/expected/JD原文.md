# Fictional Job Description — CloudExample

> **DEMO DATA ONLY.** CloudExample is a fictional company and this is not a real vacancy.

## Senior Product Manager, Billing

CloudExample is a fictional B2B SaaS company providing subscription infrastructure to digital businesses. We are looking for a Senior Product Manager to improve invoicing and subscription renewal workflows.

### Responsibilities

- Own discovery and roadmap planning for billing workflows.
- Interview customers and internal operations teams to identify workflow problems.
- Coordinate engineering, finance operations, support, sales, and data partners through delivery.
- Define measurable outcomes and use product and support data to evaluate launches.
- Document product decisions and escalation paths for internal teams.

### Requirements

- Five or more years of product management experience.
- Direct B2B SaaS experience with invoicing or subscription products.
- Evidence of customer discovery and cross-functional product delivery.
- Ability to use quantitative and qualitative evidence in product decisions.
- Working proficiency in English.

### Nice to have

- Working knowledge of SQL.
- Experience launching workflow changes in phases.

### Location

Berlin or remote in Europe.
